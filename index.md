# Starloom Privacy Policy

_Draft — last updated 15 September 2026. Contact address and publication URL to be filled in before release._

Starloom ("the app") is a single-player logic puzzle published by AppFellas. This policy explains what
information the app handles and why.

## The short version
- No account, no sign-in, no personal profile.
- Your progress, settings and statistics stay on your device.
- The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your device's advertising identifier and
  related device data, subject to the choices you make in the consent message and in your device settings.

## Information stored on your device
Level results, the daily puzzle calendar and streak, unfinished boards, settings (theme, sound, language and so
on) are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your device. No server is involved, and the app
only asks for notification permission after you choose to turn the reminder on.

Sharing a result creates an image and a short text on your device and passes them to the system share sheet.
The image never contains the puzzle's solution. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows a banner, occasional full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads. These are served by
Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads, Google may collect and process:
- your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
- ad interactions (views, taps).

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices**
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions that require it, a consent
  message from Google appears on first launch. You can change your choice at any time from
  **Settings → Privacy options** in the app.
- On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you do not allow it, the advertising
  identifier is not shared and the game works exactly the same. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Tracking.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.
- Watching one rewarded video turns banners and full-screen ads off for 30 minutes.

The app's ad requests are configured for general-audience content (rated G).

## Analytics and crash reporting
The current version of the app does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

## Children
Starloom is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum
age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion
We do not hold any personal data about you on our own servers. Data kept by Google for advertising is governed by
Google's policies. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.
